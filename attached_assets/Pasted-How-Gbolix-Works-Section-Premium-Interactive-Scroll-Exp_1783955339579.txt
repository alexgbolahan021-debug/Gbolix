How Gbolix Works Section – Premium Interactive Scroll Experience

Replace the current static "How Gbolix Works" section with a highly interactive version inspired by modern SaaS websites.

Layout

Keep the 4-step process:

01 — Submit Your Request
02 — Receive a Quote
03 — Build & Execute
04 — Deliver & Iterate

Interaction Requirements

As the user scrolls through the section:

* Step 1 activates first.
* Step 2 activates when the user scrolls further.
* Step 3 activates next.
* Step 4 activates last.

Only one step should be the primary active step at a time.

Visual Effects

Active Step:

* Full brightness.
* Slight scale increase.
* Strong border glow.
* Green → Cyan → Purple gradient accent.
* Animated icon.
* Progress line connected to the next step.

Inactive Steps:

* Lower opacity.
* Smaller scale.
* Minimal glow.
* Subtle blur reduction.

Progress Animation

Create a horizontal progress line connecting all steps.

As the user scrolls:

* The line gradually fills from left to right.
* Fill color should use Gbolix gradient:
  Green → Cyan → Purple.
* Smooth animation.
* No abrupt transitions.

Card Effects

When a step becomes active:

* Fade in.
* Slight upward movement.
* Soft glow pulse.
* Smooth transition.

Content Expansion

Each step should reveal additional details when active.

Example:

Step 1 — Submit Your Request

Show:

* Choose a service.
* Describe your goals.
* Upload files if needed.
* Submit the request.

Step 2 — Receive a Quote

Show:

* Project review.
* Scope confirmation.
* Timeline estimate.
* Pricing proposal.

Step 3 — Build & Execute

Show:

* Development begins.
* Progress tracking.
* Direct communication.
* File sharing.

Step 4 — Deliver & Iterate

Show:

* Final delivery.
* Testing and review.
* Revisions if included.
* Ongoing support options.

Motion Design

Add subtle motion throughout:

* Floating glow effects.
* Slow pulse on active icons.
* Progress line animation.
* Smooth card transitions.

No excessive movement.

Everything should feel premium and professional.

Desktop Experience

Display all four steps horizontally.

Mobile Experience

Convert into:

* Vertical timeline.
* Scroll-triggered activation.
* Progress line fills top-to-bottom.

Branding

Use Gbolix brand colors:

* Green
* Cyan
* Purple

Match the glow style used throughout the site.

Goal

The section should feel like a guided journey that visually walks visitors through the Gbolix process.

Think:

* Linear
* Stripe
* Framer
* Webflow
* Modern SaaS landing pages

This section should become one of the most visually impressive and interactive sections on the website.
