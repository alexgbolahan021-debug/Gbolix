# Gbolix Service Payment & Approval Workflow (Version 2)

Implement a complete Service Payment, Approval, Project Kickoff, Invoice, and Notification Workflow for Gbolix.

This workflow applies ONLY to Services.

Do NOT connect this workflow to Gbolix Tools, Products, Wallet, Credits, or future SaaS products.

Products will have their own instant checkout system in the future.

Maintain the existing Gbolix branding, animations, dark theme, routing, responsiveness, premium UI, and overall design language.

# STEP 8

OWNER MANUAL PAYMENT OVERRIDE

The payment workflow must NOT depend entirely on Paystack.

Users with the Owner role must have exclusive permission to override payment status.

Owner Actions:

Mark as Paid

Mark as Complimentary (Free)

Mark as Offline Payment

Mark as Discounted Payment

Cancel Payment

Every manual action must require an optional note.

Automatically store an audit log.

Example:

Payment manually approved by Owner.

Reason:

Offline Bank Transfer

If manually approved:

Payment Status:

Paid

Generate Invoice

Generate Receipt

Generate Payment Record

Move Project Status:

Planning
